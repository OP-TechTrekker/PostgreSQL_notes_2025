CLAUSES: 
- Where – filter rows based on a specified condition. 
- Select Distinct  – remove duplicate rows from the result set.
- Order By – sort the result set returned by a query. 
- Limit – retrieve a subset of rows generated by a query. 
- Like – filter data based on pattern matching.

Relational operators: < , > , <= , >= , = , !=
Logical operator: AND, OR, IN, NOT IN, BETWEEN, NOT
